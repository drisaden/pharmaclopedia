# Product specification

## Objective
Pharmaclopedia is a medicine intelligence and pharmacology learning platform for pharmacy students and healthcare learners in Nigeria. It focuses on learning pathways, safe educational explanations, and evidence-grounded medicine information.

## Core user journeys
1. Search for a medicine or therapeutic class.
2. Open a structured profile and read key information.
3. Use the demonstration tutor to learn with one of several learning modes.
4. Watch relevant educational video recommendations.
5. Compare medicines.
6. Complete a quiz and review learning points.

## Non-goals for this MVP
- Diagnosis or prescribing decisions
- Patient-facing treatment plans
- Marketplace or stock availability
- Full regulatory database integration

## Safety principles
This product is educational. It does not replace professional judgment or prescribing information. Important clinical decisions should be verified against authoritative sources.
