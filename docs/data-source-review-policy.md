# Data source and review policy

## Source standards
Pharmaclopedia uses educationally credible sources such as standard pharmacology references, essential medicines lists, and reputable institutional teaching material where appropriate. The dataset is intentionally curated rather than generated.

## Review process
- New medicine entries should be reviewed before inclusion.
- Medicine profiles and quiz content must be cross-checked against authoritative sources.
- Any uncertain or unverified field should be excluded or labeled as not yet verified.

## Prohibited practices
- Inventing manufacturers, brands, approvals, or registrations
- Copying large sections of reference material without attribution
- Presenting unreviewed AI text as verified medical fact
- Claiming a medicine is suitable for every patient
